# AI Workplace Productivity Assistant

## Overview
Build a polished, responsive single-page productivity dashboard with three client-side AI-style tools: an email writer, task planner, and workplace chatbot. No account screens, data storage, or backend services will be added.

## Experience
- Responsive sidebar on desktop and compact top navigation on mobile.
- Overview header with clear navigation between Email Writer, Task Planner, and AI Assistant.
- Smart Email Generator with purpose, recipient, context, selectable tone, loading state, editable generated draft, copy, and reset controls.
- AI Task Planner with daily/weekly mode, task input, generated prioritized schedule, editable task details, completion toggles, and priority indicators.
- AI Chatbot with prompt suggestions, conversational messages, typing feedback, and a working message composer.
- Visible Responsible AI Disclaimer at the bottom of every workspace.

## Visual Direction
- Professional blue, white, and soft-grey palette with restrained borders and shadows.
- Crisp SaaS dashboard hierarchy, compact controls, and accessible contrast.
- Responsive behavior optimized for wide desktops, tablets, and small phones.
- Subtle transitions and reduced-motion support.

## Technical Details
- Keep all generation deterministic and browser-only, clearly labeling results as AI-generated.
- Use existing React, Tailwind, Lucide icons, and design-system controls.
- Define reusable semantic color and typography tokens in the global stylesheet.
- Add route-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Verify the generated flows and desktop/mobile presentation in the running preview.
